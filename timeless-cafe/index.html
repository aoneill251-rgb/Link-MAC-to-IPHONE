Our Menu

Customer favourites

From brunch classics to something completely different — our menu changes with the seasons and always has something to discover.

🥔
Signature
Bacon Hash
Our most-loved dish — crispy potato hash topped with bacon, poached eggs and a house sauce. People drive from Dublin just for this.
🌶
Lunch
Cajun Chicken Ciabatta
Juicy spiced chicken on freshly baked ciabatta with chips and coffee — a full lunch under €15. A repeat offender for our regulars.
🍳
Brunch
Mozzarella Hash
Golden potato hash loaded with melted mozzarella, caramelised onions and a perfectly poached egg. Vegetarian and utterly indulgent.
🐟
Brunch
Salmon Hash
Silky Atlantic salmon on crispy hash with hollandaise and fresh herbs. Elegant enough for a special occasion, priced for every day.
🍞
Brunch
Sourdough Breakfast
Thick-cut sourdough with cumin hummus, poached eggs and seasonal toppings. Inspired, light and full of flavour.
🥘
Special
Korean Coriander Soup
A bold, warming soup with Korean-inspired spicing that has earned its own cult following. The kind of thing that turns a visit into a regular habit.
