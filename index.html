About Us

Passion for hair, dedication to you

H5 Hair is Janet Weldon's vision of what a hair salon should be — a place where you feel heard, valued, and leave looking truly your best. With years of experience crafting beautiful hair, Janet has built a reputation for exceptional colour, precision cuts and a warmth that keeps clients coming back.

Every appointment is tailored to you. Whether you're after a subtle refresh, a bold transformation, or a stunning bridal look, the H5 Hair team brings skill and care to every strand.
