About Us

A salon built on listening & loving what we do

H5 Hair by Janet Weldon has been the go-to hair studio in Maynooth for years, built on a genuine love of the craft and a real commitment to making every client feel truly heard. Janet and the team take time with every single visit — whether you're after a transformative colour, flawless extensions, or a perfect blow-dry, you leave looking and feeling your best.

With a warm, relaxed atmosphere and a team that genuinely cares, it's no surprise clients keep coming back year after year.
