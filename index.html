About H5 Hair

Passion for hair,
dedication to you

H5 Hair is Janet Weldon's vision of what a hair salon should be — a place where you feel genuinely heard and leave looking truly your best. Located in the heart of Maynooth with easy, convenient parking, we've built a reputation for exceptional colour, precision cuts, and a warmth that keeps clients coming back year after year.

Every appointment is tailored to you. Whether you're after a subtle refresh, a bold transformation, or a breathtaking bridal look, Janet and the team bring skill, care, and artistry to every strand.
