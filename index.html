About Us

A salon built on craft & care

Shear Elegance has been transforming hair and boosting confidence in Dublin since 2018. Our team of experienced stylists believe every visit should leave you feeling your absolute best — whether you're coming in for a quick trim or a complete colour transformation.

We use only premium, ethically sourced products and stay up to date with the latest techniques so you always leave with a look you love.
