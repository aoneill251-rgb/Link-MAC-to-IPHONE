Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 May 2026

Shear Elegance ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal data and complying with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Irish data protection law.

1. Who We Are

Shear Elegance operates from 14 Grafton Lane, Dublin 2, Ireland. We are the data controller for the personal data collected through this website. You can contact us at hello@shearelegance.ie.

2. What Data We Collect

When you submit a booking request, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Your preferred appointment date and service
  • Any additional notes you provide

We also collect basic usage data through cookies (see Section 6).

3. Why We Process Your Data

We process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract / pre-contractual steps — to respond to your booking request and confirm your appointment.
  • Consent — for cookie usage where required.
  • Legitimate interests — to improve our website and prevent fraud.

4. How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain booking inquiry data for up to 12 months, after which it is securely deleted. If you become a regular client, we retain relevant records as required to provide our service and comply with any legal obligations.

5. Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share data with trusted service providers who process it on our behalf (e.g. email delivery services), under strict data processing agreements.

6. Cookies

We use cookies to remember your cookie consent preference. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. You can manage cookie preferences at any time through your browser settings.

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request erasure of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Commission (Ireland): dataprotection.ie

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@shearelegance.ie. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

This website is served over HTTPS. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access or disclosure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at hello@shearelegance.ie or write to us at 14 Grafton Lane, Dublin 2, Ireland.
